After you pair with a device, you can connect to it without pairing again.

Make sure Bluetooth is on ([learn how](/bluetooth/turn-the-bluetooth-feature-on-off)), then do one of the following:

- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Bluetooth** > tap the device name.
- From any screen: Open the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu) > **Bluetooth** > tap the device name.
