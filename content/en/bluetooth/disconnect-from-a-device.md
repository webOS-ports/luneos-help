Do one of the following:

- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Bluetooth** > tap the connected device name.
- From any screen: Open the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu) > **Bluetooth** > tap the connected device name.

Disconnecting does not remove the pairing. You can connect again at any time ([learn how](/bluetooth/connect-with-a-paired-device)).
