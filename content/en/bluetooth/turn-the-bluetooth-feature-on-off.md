- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Bluetooth** > tap the switch at the top of the screen.
- From any screen: Open the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu) > **Bluetooth** > **Turn on Bluetooth** or **Turn off Bluetooth**.
