Your device can connect to the headset or speaker you used last when Bluetooth comes on. Not every device has this option yet.

**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Bluetooth** > turn **Reconnect headsets automatically** on or off.

A device you disconnect in the Bluetooth app is not connected again until you connect it yourself.
