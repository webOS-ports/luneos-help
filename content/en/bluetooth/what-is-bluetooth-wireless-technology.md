With the Bluetooth wireless technology on your device, you can connect to other Bluetooth devices, such as a headset.

Before you can connect to another Bluetooth device, you need to pair with that device. You can make the pairing request from your device ([learn how](/bluetooth/make-a-pairing-request-to-another-device)) or accept a request from the other device ([learn how](/bluetooth/accept-a-pairing-request-from-another-device)).
