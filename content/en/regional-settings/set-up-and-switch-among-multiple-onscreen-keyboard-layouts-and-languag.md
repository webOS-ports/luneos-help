1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Regional Settings**.
2. Under **Keyboards**, do any of the following:
   - To add a keyboard: Tap **Add...** > tap a language.
   - To choose the keyboard that comes up first: Tap it. **Default** appears beside it.
   - To remove a keyboard: Swipe it sideways > **Remove Keyboard**.

To switch language while you type: Tap the language key on the keyboard > tap a language. The key appears when more than one keyboard is set up.

To change the layout, see [Use another keyboard layout](/text-assist/use-another-keyboard-layout).
