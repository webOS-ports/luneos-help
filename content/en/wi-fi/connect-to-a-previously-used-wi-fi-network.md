Your device automatically connects to known networks, so you don't usually have to do anything. Turn on Wi-Fi if it is off ([learn how](/wi-fi/turn-wi-fi-on-off "Turn Wi-Fi on/off")).

To use a different known network: **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi** > tap the network name.

To stop your device from joining a known network by itself: **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi** > touch and hold the network name > turn off **Connect automatically** > **Done**.
