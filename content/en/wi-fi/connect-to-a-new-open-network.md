1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi**.
2. Make sure Wi-Fi is on ([learn how](/wi-fi/turn-wi-fi-on-off "Turn Wi-Fi on/off")).
3. Under **Choose a network**, tap the network name. Open networks have no padlock beside their name.

You can also open the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu), tap **Wi-Fi**, and tap the network name there.
