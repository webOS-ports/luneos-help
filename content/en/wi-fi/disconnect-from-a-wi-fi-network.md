- To disconnect and keep the network's settings: **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi** > tap the connected network. You can also turn off Wi-Fi ([learn how](/wi-fi/turn-wi-fi-on-off "Turn Wi-Fi on/off")).
- To remove the network and its passphrase from your device: **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi** > touch and hold the network name > **Forget network**.
