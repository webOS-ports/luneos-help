1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Wi-Fi**.
2. Make sure Wi-Fi is on ([learn how](/wi-fi/turn-wi-fi-on-off "Turn Wi-Fi on/off")).
3. Under **Choose a network**, tap the network name. Secure networks have a padlock beside their name.
4. Enter the passphrase, and the username if the network asks for one. To see what you type, tap **Show passphrase**.
5. Tap **Connect**.

While the device connects, **connecting...** appears next to the network name. A check mark appears when it is connected.

If it doesn't connect, see [If a Wi-Fi network won't connect](/wi-fi/if-a-wi-fi-network-wont-connect).
