1. [Display Card view](/basics/display-card-view-minimize-an-application) and tap the **Just type...** field.
2. Type what you're looking for.
3. To see only results from your applications, tap the **Content** tab.
4. Tap one of the following. The number shows how many matches there are.

   **Email:** Tap a message to view it.

   **Calendar Events:** Tap an event to open it.

   **Bookmarks & History:** Tap an entry to go to the website.

   **Songs:** Tap a song to open it in Music.

To choose where Just Type looks, see [Customize Just Type](/just-type/customize-just-type).
