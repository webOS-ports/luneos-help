1. [Display Card view](/basics/display-card-view-minimize-an-application) and tap the **Just type...** field.
2. Type the application's name or a related keyword.
3. When it appears under **Launch**, tap it.

{if:keyboard}On a physical keyboard, you can just start typing in Card view.
