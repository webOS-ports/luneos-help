To delete one picture or video:

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap an album > tap the thumbnail.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the screen.
3. Tap the Delete button > **Delete**.

To delete several:

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap the album.
2. Tap the Select button at the lower left of the album.
3. Tap the thumbnails you want to delete, or tap **Select All**.
4. Tap the Delete button > **Delete**.

Deleting something from a cloud album deletes it from your cloud account as well, and this cannot be undone.
