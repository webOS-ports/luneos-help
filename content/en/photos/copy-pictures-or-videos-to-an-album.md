To copy several at once:

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos**.
2. Tap the album with the pictures or videos.
3. Tap the Select button at the lower left of the album.
4. Tap the thumbnails you want, or tap **Select All**.
5. Tap the Add to Album button.
6. Tap an album, or tap **Create New Album** > enter a name > **Create Album**.

To copy one:

1. Open the picture or video. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the screen.
2. Tap the More button > **Add to Album**.
3. Tap an album, or tap **Create New Album** > enter a name > **Create Album**.

Videos cannot go into a cloud album, and nothing can be added to Photo roll.
