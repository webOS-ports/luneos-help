1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap an album > tap a picture.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the picture.
3. Tap the More button > **Set As Wallpaper**.

The message **Wallpaper set** appears. The picture is scaled and cropped to the screen for you; there is no step to choose the part to use.
