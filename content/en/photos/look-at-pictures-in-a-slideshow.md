1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos**.
2. Do one of the following:
   - To play an album: Tap the album > tap the Slideshow button at the lower right.
   - To start from a picture: Tap the album > tap the picture > tap the Slideshow button in the bar at the top. If the bar is hidden, tap the picture.
   - To play the whole library: Tap the Slideshow button in the library's toolbar.
3. Swipe left or right to go on or back.
4. To end the slideshow, tap the screen.

The screen stays on during a slideshow. Each picture shows for 10 seconds unless you changed the time in Exhibition ([learn how](/exhibition/view-a-picture-slideshow)).
