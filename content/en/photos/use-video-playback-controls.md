A video in Photos & Videos starts by itself where you left it. Tap the screen to show or hide the controls.

- **Play/pause:** The button at the left of the bar at the bottom.
- **Move forward or back:** Drag the slider. The time played and the time left are on either side of it.
- **Fit or fill:** The button at the right of the bar switches between the whole picture and filling the screen.
- **Go back:** Tap the back arrow at the top left.

Swipe left or right to go to the next or previous item in the album.

{if:volumebutton}Use the volume buttons on your device to change the sound level.
