1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap an album > tap the picture or video.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the screen.
3. Tap the Share button. Email opens a new message with the file attached.
4. Address the message and send it.

Email is the only way to share from Photos & Videos.
