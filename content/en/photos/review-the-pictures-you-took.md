1. In Camera, tap the thumbnail at the end of the control bar. Or open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Photos & Videos**.

   This opens Photos & Videos at its library, not at the picture you just took.
2. Tap **Photo roll**. The newest picture comes first.
3. Tap a thumbnail, then swipe left or right to see the others.
