1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos**.
2. Tap an album from a cloud account > tap a picture.
3. If the bar at the top of the screen is hidden, tap the picture.
4. Tap the Comments button. It appears only where the account has comments.
5. Tap **Tap here to add comment**, enter your comment, and press Enter.
6. Tap the Comments button again to close the panel.

Videos have no comments.
