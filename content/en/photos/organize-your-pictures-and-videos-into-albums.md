Photos & Videos lists libraries and albums.

Under **LIBRARIES** you find **All Photos & Videos**, **My Device** for what is stored on the device, and one entry for each cloud account with pictures. Tap **LIBRARIES** to show or hide this list.

Below it are the albums of the library you chose. Each folder on the device with pictures or videos in it is an album.

{if:camera}Pictures you take are in **Photo roll**.

- To make an album: [Create an album](/photos/create-an-album).
- To put pictures in an album: [Copy pictures or videos to an album](/photos/copy-pictures-or-videos-to-an-album).
- To rename an album: Open it and tap its name at the top of the screen. Photo roll and cloud albums cannot be renamed.
- To delete an album: [Delete an album](/photos/delete-an-album).
