1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos**.
2. Tap an album > tap a thumbnail. The newest pictures come first.
3. Swipe left or right to see other pictures in the album.
4. To zoom, pinch or double-tap the picture, and drag to move around in it.
5. To go back to the album, tap the screen if the bar at the top is hidden, then tap the back arrow at its left end.

{if:camera}The **Photo roll** album holds the pictures you took with the camera.

Photos & Videos shows the pictures and videos in your device's storage, and those in cloud accounts you have set up ([learn how](/photos/set-up-an-online-pictures-or-videos-account-on-your-device)). To add pictures from a computer, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).
