To upload pictures, add them to an album in a cloud account. You need a cloud account set up first ([learn how](/photos/set-up-an-online-pictures-or-videos-account-on-your-device)).

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap the album > tap a picture.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the picture.
3. Tap the More button > **Add to Album**.
4. Tap an album from your cloud account.

To upload several pictures, use the Select button in the album ([learn how](/photos/copy-pictures-or-videos-to-an-album)). Videos cannot be uploaded this way.
