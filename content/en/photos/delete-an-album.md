Only an empty album can be deleted.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap the album.
2. Delete the pictures and videos in it ([learn how](/photos/delete-pictures-or-videos)).
3. In the empty album, tap **Delete Album** > **Delete Album**.

The button does not appear for albums that cannot be deleted.
