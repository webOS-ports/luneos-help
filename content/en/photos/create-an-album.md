1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos**.
2. Tap **New Album** at the bottom of the list of libraries and albums, or open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **New Album**.
3. Enter the album name > **Create Album**.

The new album is empty. Open it and tap **Add Photos** to pick pictures and videos for it.
