1. Connect your device to your computer with a USB cable.
2. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) USB** > **Connect as media device**.
3. On your computer, copy the audio file to the **ringtones** folder on your device ([learn how](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer)).

Supported file types are MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, and WMA.
