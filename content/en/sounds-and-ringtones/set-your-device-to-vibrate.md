1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Sounds & Ringtones**.
2. Under **Vibrate**, set either or both of the following:

   **Sounds on:** Tap **Sound & Vibrate** or **Sound**.

   **Sounds off:** Tap **Vibrate** or **Mute**.

These settings stay available while sounds are off.
