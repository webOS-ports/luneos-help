Your device comes with a set of system sounds. You can add your own ringtones by putting audio files in the ringtones folder on your device ([learn how](/sounds-and-ringtones/copy-a-ringtone-to-your-device)). These file types are listed: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, and WMA.

Files in that folder appear under **My ringtones** in the tone list. To use one, see [Select a file from the ringtones folder as a ringtone](/sounds-and-ringtones/select-a-file-from-the-ringtones-folder-as-a-ringtone).
