1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Sounds & Ringtones**.
2. Tap **Ringtone** to open the list of tones.
3. Tap a tone under **My ringtones** or **System sounds**. The tone plays briefly.
4. To set the volume, drag the **Ringtone Volume** slider.

You pick the **Alert Tone** and **Notification Tone** the same way, under **Alerts**.
