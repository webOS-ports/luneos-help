In Documents, use the bar at the bottom of the screen, or open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)**:

- **Zoom:** Tap the zoom buttons or pinch. Tap the percentage to fit the page again.
- **Find:** Tap **Find** and type. Use the arrows beside the field to move between matches.
- **Night Reading:** Light text on dark pages. Tap **Day Reading** to go back.
- **Document Info:** The file's size and folder, and its title, author and dates.
- **Freeze First Row:** In a workbook, keeps the top row in view. Tap **Unfreeze First Row** to undo it.

If you tap a link, Documents asks before it opens the browser.
