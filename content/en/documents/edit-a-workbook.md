Documents never changes the original. You save your changes as a new file beside it.

1. Open a workbook and tap a cell.
2. Tap **Edit**, or tap the cell again.
3. Type a value or a formula, and tap **Done**.
4. When you have finished, tap **Save a Copy**, check the name, and confirm.

Very large workbooks open read-only.
