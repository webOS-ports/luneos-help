Documents reads Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, .xls), PowerPoint (.pptx), OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp), CSV, plain text and Markdown files.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Documents**. It lists every document in your internal storage, with the recent ones first.
2. To narrow the list, tap **Text**, **Sheets** or **Slides**, or type in **Search**.
3. Tap a document.

To go back to the list, use the back gesture.

If a new document isn't listed, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Look for Documents Again**. To add documents from a computer, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).

Older .doc, .ppt and .rtf files open only on devices with LibreOffice's service, which isn't installed on every device. PDF files open in [PDF Viewer](/pdf/open-a-file).
