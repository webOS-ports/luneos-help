1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > **Playlists**.
2. Press and hold the playlist, or tap the More button at the end of its row.
3. Tap **Delete** > **Delete**.

The songs stay in your library. Playlists under **AUTOMATIC** cannot be deleted.
