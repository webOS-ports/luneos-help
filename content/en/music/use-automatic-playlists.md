Music keeps these lists for you. They are at the top of **Playlists**, under **AUTOMATIC**. Tap one to play it. You can't edit them.

- **Favorites:** Songs you marked with the heart.
- **Recently Added:** The songs added in the last 30 days, or at least the newest 25.
- **Recently Played:** What you played last.
- **Most Played:** What you play most.
- **Never Played:** What you haven't played yet.
- **Memory Lane:** Songs you played often once, but not in the last two months.

These lists hold only songs on your device.
