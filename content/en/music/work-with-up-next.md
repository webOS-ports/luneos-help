Up Next is the list of songs Music is playing, in order.

To add to it: Tap and hold a song or an album, then tap one of these:

- **Play Next:** It plays right after the current song.
- **Add to Queue:** It plays after everything else.

To see it, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Up Next**, or tap the Up Next button in Now Playing. Then:

- To jump to a song: Tap it.
- To save the list: Tap **Save as Playlist** (**Save** on a small screen).
- To remove what is still to come: Tap **Clear Up Next** (**Clear** on a small screen).

To reorder or remove single songs, see [Change the order of songs](/music/change-order-of-items-in-the-playlist-view) and [Remove a song](/music/remove-a-song-from-playlist-view).

Music remembers Up Next and your place in it when you close it.
