- In Now Playing: Tap the heart. Tap it again to take the song off.
- In a list: Tap and hold a song, then tap **Add to Favorites** or **Remove from Favorites**.

To see your favorites: Tap **Playlists** > **Favorites**.

Only songs on your device are listed in **Favorites**. Streamed songs you mark stay marked, but they aren't in that list.
