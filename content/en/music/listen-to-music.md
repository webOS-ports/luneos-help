1. Add music to your device ([learn how](/music/get-music-onto-your-device)).
2. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music**.
3. Tap a category: **Albums**, **Artists**, **Songs**, **Genres**, **Playlists** or **Folders**. If your device has streaming, there is also **Streaming** ([learn how](/music/stream-music)). On a phone the categories are tabs at the top; on a tablet they are on the left.
4. Do one of the following:
   - To play a song and the ones after it: Tap the song.
   - To play an album, artist or playlist: Open it and tap **Play** or **Shuffle**.
   - To play everything at random: Tap **Shuffle** in the bar above the list. On a small screen, tap the Sort and shuffle button beside the tabs > **Shuffle All**.

Music keeps playing when you close its card, switch to another app or turn off the screen. While it plays, controls appear in the notification area.

{if:modem}Music pauses for a phone call and carries on afterward. You can change this in Preferences.

To stop the music, pause it, or swipe away its notification.
