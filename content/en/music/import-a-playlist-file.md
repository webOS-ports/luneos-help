Music reads M3U, M3U8 and PLS playlist files.

1. Copy the playlist file to your device ([learn how](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer)), with the songs it lists.
2. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > tap **Folders**.
3. Go to the folder and tap the file.
4. Tap **Import as Playlist**.

Music tells you how many songs it found. To play the file without keeping it, tap **Play** instead.
