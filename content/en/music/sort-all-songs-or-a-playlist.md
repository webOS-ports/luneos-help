1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > **Songs** or **Albums**.
2. Tap **Sort:** in the bar above the list. On a small screen, tap the Sort and shuffle button beside the tabs instead.
3. Tap how you want to sort. For songs: **Title**, **Artist**, **Album**, **Recently added**, **Year** or **Length**. For albums: **Title**, **Artist**, **Year** or **Recently added**.

Playlists keep their own order. To change it, see [Change the order of songs](/music/change-order-of-items-in-the-playlist-view).
