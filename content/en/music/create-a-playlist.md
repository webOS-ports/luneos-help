1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > **Playlists** > **New Playlist**.
2. Enter a name and confirm.

Your playlist appears under **YOUR PLAYLISTS**. To add songs, see [Add music to a playlist](/music/add-music-to-a-playlist).
