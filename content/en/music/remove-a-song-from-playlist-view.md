1. In Music, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Up Next**.
2. Tap the cross on the song's row.

This does not delete the file or change your playlists. It only takes the song out of what is coming up. To empty the list, tap **Clear Up Next**.
