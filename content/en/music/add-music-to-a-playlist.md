1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music**.
2. Press and hold a song or album. You can also tap the More button at the end of a song's row.
3. Tap **Add to Playlist…**.
4. Tap a playlist, or tap **New Playlist…** to make one.

To put everything in Up Next into a playlist, open Up Next and tap **Save as Playlist**.
