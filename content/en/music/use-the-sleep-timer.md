1. Start playing music.
2. Open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Sleep Timer**, or tap the Sleep Timer button in Now Playing.
3. Tap **15 Minutes**, **30 Minutes**, **45 Minutes**, **60 Minutes** or **90 Minutes**, or **End of This Song**.

The music fades out over the last 30 seconds, then pauses.

To see how long is left, or to cancel, tap **Sleep Timer** again. To cancel, tap **Turn Off**.
