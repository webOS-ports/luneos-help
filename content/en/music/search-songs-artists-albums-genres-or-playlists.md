1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music**.
2. Tap the Search button at the top of the screen.
3. Start typing in the **Artists, albums, songs** field. Results appear as you type.
4. Tap a result.

Below the songs on your device, you see results from your [streaming services](/music/play-from-a-streaming-service).

To close the search, use the back gesture.
