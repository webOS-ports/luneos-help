1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > **Playlists** > tap the playlist.
2. Press and hold a song, or tap the More button at the end of its row.
3. Tap **Remove from Playlist**.

The song stays in your library and in any other playlist it is in. You can do this only in playlists you made.
