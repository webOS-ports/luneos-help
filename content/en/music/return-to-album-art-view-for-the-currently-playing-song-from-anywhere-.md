While a song plays, a small player sits at the bottom of the Music app. Tap it, or swipe it up, to open Now Playing. You can also open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Now Playing**.

To leave Now Playing, swipe down on the cover or tap the arrow at the top.
