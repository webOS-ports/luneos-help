1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music**.
2. Open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Preferences**.
3. Change any of these:
   - **Volume levelling:** Makes songs play about as loud as each other, if their files have ReplayGain tags. Choose **Off**, **Automatic**, **Track** or **Album**.
   - **Prevent clipping:** Keeps levelled songs from distorting.
   - **Crossfade:** Fades one song into the next, for 2 to 12 seconds. **Off (gapless)** leaves no gap between songs.
   - **Resume audiobooks and long tracks:** Audiobooks, podcasts and tracks over 20 minutes start where you left them.
   - {if:modem}**Pause for calls** and **Carry on after the call**.
   - **Colours from the album cover:** Now Playing takes its colors from the cover.
4. If songs are missing, tap **Reload Library**. If a cover is wrong, tap **Forget Covers**.

Preferences also holds the switches for [lyrics](/music/read-the-lyrics-of-a-song) and [artist information](/music/read-about-the-artist).
