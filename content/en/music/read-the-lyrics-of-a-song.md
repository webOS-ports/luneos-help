1. Open Now Playing and tap the Lyrics button.
2. The lyrics take the place of the cover. If they have timing, the line being sung lights up. Tap a line to go there in the song.
3. To go back to the cover, tap the Lyrics button again.

Music uses lyrics stored in the song, or in a .lrc file with the same name beside it.

If a song has none, or it is streamed, most copies of Music look it up on LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and send the artist and title. To turn this off, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences** and turn off **Look up lyrics online**.
