1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Music** > tap **Playlists**.
2. Under **YOUR PLAYLISTS**, tap and hold a playlist, then tap **Export to Playlists**.

Music saves it as an M3U8 file in the Playlists folder of your internal storage, where you can reach it from a computer. Streamed songs are left out.
