Now Playing shows the album cover. Buttons on the screen show other things:

- To see the songs coming up: Tap the Up Next button.
- To see the lyrics: Tap the Lyrics button ([learn how](/music/read-the-lyrics-of-a-song)).
- To read about the artist: Tap the About the artist button ([learn how](/music/read-about-the-artist)).

To go back to the cover, tap the same button again.
