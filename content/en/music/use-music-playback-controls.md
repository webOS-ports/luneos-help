These are in Now Playing:

- **Play/pause:** The middle button, or tap the middle of the cover.
- **Next or previous song:** The buttons on either side, swipe the cover sideways, or tap the cover's right or left edge.
- **Fast forward or rewind:** Press and hold the next or previous button.
- **Move in a song:** Drag the seek bar.
- **Shuffle:** Tap Shuffle to play the rest in random order.
- **Repeat:** Tap Repeat to go between off, all and one.
- **Favorite:** Tap the heart. The song goes in the **Favorites** playlist.
- **Sleep timer:** Tap the Sleep Timer button to stop after some time, or at **End of This Song**.

Audiobooks, podcasts and tracks over 20 minutes carry on where you left them. You can change this in Preferences, with **Resume audiobooks and long tracks**.

{if:volumebutton}Use the volume buttons on your device to change the sound level.
