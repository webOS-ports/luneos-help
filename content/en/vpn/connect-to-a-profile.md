1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) VPN**.
2. Tap a profile. The line under its name shows **Connecting...** and then **Connected**.
3. If you are asked for more information, enter it and tap **Send**.

You can connect more than one profile at a time.
