1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) VPN**.
2. Tap **Edit** beside the profile.
3. Edit your settings > **Save**.

You can't change a profile's name or connection type. To keep a stored password, leave a field that shows **(unchanged)** as it is.
