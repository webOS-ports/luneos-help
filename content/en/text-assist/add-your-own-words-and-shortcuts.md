1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Text Assist** > **Edit User Dictionary**.
2. To edit your words, tap **Words** and do any of the following:
   - To add a word: Enter the word in **Add a word** > **Add**. Words you add are never corrected.
   - To delete a word: Swipe the word sideways > **Delete**.
3. To edit your shortcuts, tap **Shortcuts** and do any of the following:
   - To add a shortcut: Enter the shortcut and the text it stands for > **Add**.
   - To delete a shortcut: Swipe the shortcut sideways > **Delete**.
4. Tap **Done**.

**NOTE:** The keyboard does not expand shortcuts yet. Shortcuts you add are stored for when it does.
