1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Text Assist**.
2. Do any of the following:
   - Under **Text Correction**, turn **Auto-Capitalization**, **Auto-Correction**, **Spell Checking**, or **Predictive Text** on or off.
   - Under **Keyboard**, tap **Layout** to choose a keyboard layout. Some layouts are for phones or tablets only.
   - Under **Keyboard**, tap **Size** > **Extra small**, **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**.

If a physical keyboard is attached, a **Hardware Keyboard** group also appears ([learn how](/text-assist/set-hardware-keyboard-options)).

The languages the keyboard offers are set in Regional Settings ([learn how](/regional-settings/set-up-and-switch-among-multiple-onscreen-keyboard-layouts-and-languag)). To turn key sounds on or off, see [Set sound options](/sounds-and-ringtones/set-sound-options).
