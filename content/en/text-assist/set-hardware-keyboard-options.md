These options appear when your device has a physical keyboard or one is attached.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Text Assist**.
2. Under **Hardware Keyboard**, do any of the following:

   - **Layout:** Tap to match the letters on your keyboard. **Automatic** uses the layout your device detected.
   - **On-Screen Keyboard:** Turn on to reach characters the keys don't have. When this is off, the onscreen keyboard stays out of the way while the physical keyboard is in use.
   - **Type on the Keypad:** Appears on a keypad with digits and no letters. Turn on to type words by pressing a key repeatedly. If you leave it off, the onscreen keyboard stays.

You can also show or hide the onscreen keyboard from the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu): Tap **Show On-screen Keyboard** or **Hide On-screen Keyboard**.
