1. Go to Card view ([learn how](/basics/display-card-view-minimize-an-application)) and open an application from the Launcher.
2. To return to the call screen, go to Card view and tap the Phone card.

Don't throw the Phone card off the screen while you are on a call: closing Phone ends the call.
