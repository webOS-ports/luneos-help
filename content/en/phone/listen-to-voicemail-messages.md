Do one of the following:

- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > press and hold **1** on the dial pad.
- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Call Voicemail**.
- Tap the new voicemail notification.

Phone calls your voicemail system, and you listen to your messages by following its voice prompts. If you see **Unable to find voicemail number.**, enter the number ([learn how](/phone/change-your-voicemail-number)).
