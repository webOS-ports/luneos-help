1. Make or answer a first call, and then make or answer a second call. Each call has its own line on the call card.
2. Tap the merge button on a call line to create the conference call.
3. Do any of the following:
   - To see who is on the call: The call card lists each caller.
   - To hang up on one caller, or to take one aside to talk privately: Tap the caller's row, and then tap one of the two buttons under it.
   - To split the conference call: Tap **Split**.
4. To end the conference call, tap the disconnect button at the bottom of the call card.
