1. Connect the headset. Calls go to the headset by themselves.
2. Use the button on the headset:
   - Answer an incoming call or a call waiting: Press the button.
   - Ignore an incoming call: Press and hold the button.
   - Hang up: Press the button with no call ringing.
3. If you want to stop using the headset, do either of the following:
   - Use the speakerphone: Tap **Speaker**.
   - Use your device without the headset: Disconnect the headset.
