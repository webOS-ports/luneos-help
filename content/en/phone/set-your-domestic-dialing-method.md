This setting appears only if you have an account that can place calls besides your SIM card. [Add the account in Accounts](/accounts/use-the-accounts-application-to-set-up-an-online-account) first.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences** > **Domestic Calls** > **Use**.
2. Tap one of the following:
   - To use your wireless service provider for domestic calls: Tap the name of your provider.
   - To use another account: Tap the account.
   - To be asked which service to use every time you make a domestic call: Tap **Always Ask**.
