**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences** > under **Calls**, turn **Call Waiting** on or off.

Check with your wireless service provider about availability and pricing of the call waiting feature.
