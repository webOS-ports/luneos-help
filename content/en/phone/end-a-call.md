Do one of the following:

- Tap the disconnect button at the bottom of the call card. With more than one call, this ends all of them, and each call also has a disconnect button of its own.
- Go to Card view and throw the Phone card off the top of the screen. Closing Phone ends every call.
- If a wired headset is attached, press the button on the headset.
