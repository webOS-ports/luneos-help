Do any of the following:

- Put the call on speakerphone: Tap **Speaker**. Tap it again to go back. If a Bluetooth device is connected, the button says **Audio**, and each tap moves to the next audio route.
- Mute the microphone so you can't be heard: Tap **Mute**. **MUTE** appears beside the call time. Tap **Mute** again to unmute.
- Display the dial pad to enter numbers for navigating phone trees, responding to prompts, and so on: Tap **Dialpad**.
- Add a second call: [**Learn how**](/phone/make-a-second-call).
- {if:volumebutton}Adjust call volume: Press the **volume** buttons on your device.
