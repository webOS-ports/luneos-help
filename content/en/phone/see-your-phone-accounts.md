Phone accounts are what your device can place calls over: your SIM card, and any chat account that can make calls.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Accounts**.
2. Do any of the following:
   - See each account: The line under its name shows the signed-in account, or **Ready** or **Not available**.
   - Add a calling account: Tap **Add an account** ([see which accounts can make calls](/chat-apps/call-someone-over-a-chat-account)).
3. Tap **Done**.
