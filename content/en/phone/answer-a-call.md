Do one of the following:

- Tap the answer button on the left of the incoming call. It looks the same when the Phone app is open, when you are in another app, and when the screen is locked, so you don't have to unlock the screen first.
- If a wired headset is attached, press the button on the headset.

To open the caller's contact entry instead, tap the name of a caller who is in Contacts.
