**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **Favorites** > tap the favorite. The call goes out to the number shown under the name, which is the contact's primary number.

To use another number, tap the arrow at the right of the row > tap the number.

You first need to make the contact a favorite ([learn how](/contacts/create-a-favorite)).
