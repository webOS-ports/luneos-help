1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences**.
2. Under **Calls**, turn **Call Forwarding** on or off.
3. When Call Forwarding is on, tap **Enter Number** and enter the number to forward your calls to. Tap the number to change it.

Check with your wireless service provider about availability and pricing of forwarded calls; additional charges may apply.

To forward only some calls, such as when you're busy, use a [network service code](/phone/use-network-service-codes).
