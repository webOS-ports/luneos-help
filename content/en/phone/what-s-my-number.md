**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences**.

If your phone number is stored on your SIM card, it appears under **Phone Number** at the top of the Phone Preferences screen. Not all wireless service providers put phone number information on the SIM card.
