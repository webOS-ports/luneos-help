Favorites are kept in Contacts, so **+ Add Favorite** takes you there.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **Favorites** > **+ Add Favorite**. Contacts opens.
2. Tap **Favourites** > **Add Favorite** > tap the contact.

See also [Dial a favorite](/phone/dial-a-favorite)
