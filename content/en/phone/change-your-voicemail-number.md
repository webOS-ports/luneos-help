1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences**.
2. Under **Voicemail Number**, tap the number and enter the new one.
3. Tap **Done**.

The number normally comes from your SIM card. If your wireless service provider changes the number of its voicemail system, change it here too. You need a SIM card and a network connection to edit this field.
