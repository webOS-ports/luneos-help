**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Phone Preferences** > under **Calls**, turn **Show My Caller ID** on or off.

Check with your wireless service provider about availability and pricing of the caller ID feature. This setting does not affect whether your device displays the caller ID for an incoming call.

To hide your caller ID for a single call, see [Hide your caller ID for one call](/phone/hide-your-caller-id-for-one-call).
