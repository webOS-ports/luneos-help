1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Contacts** > **Favourites** > tap the contact.
2. Tap the star to turn it off.

The contact leaves the **Favorites** tab in Phone as well. When you remove a contact from your favorites, you do not delete the contact itself.
