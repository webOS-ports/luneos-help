1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Phone** > **Call Log**.
2. Do any of the following:
   - Switch the types of calls displayed: Tap **All** or **Missed**.
   - Find a call: Enter a name or number in **Filter**.
   - Call back: Tap the entry.
   - Display the individual calls and more options: Tap the arrow at the right of the entry.
   - Delete an entry: Drag it to the left > **Delete**.
   - Clear call history: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Clear Call History** > **OK**.
3. If you displayed the options, do any of the following:
   - View the contact entry: Tap **View Contact**.
   - Save the number to Contacts: Tap **Add to Contacts** ([learn how](/phone/save-a-phone-number-to-contacts)).
