Certificates are digital documents that prove who a website, server, or network is. Your workplace may ask you to install one.

Certificate Manager is installed, but it does not have an icon in the Launcher yet.

- [Install a certificate](/device-info/install-a-certificate-from-usb-drive-storage)
- [View certificate details](/device-info/view-certificate-details)
- [Delete a certificate](/device-info/delete-a-certificate)

A VPN profile keeps its own certificates. See [Add an OpenVPN VPN profile](/vpn/add-an-openvpn-vpn-profile).
