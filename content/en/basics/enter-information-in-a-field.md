Tap a field and type, or paste text you copied earlier. To accept what you entered, do one of the following:

- Tap outside the field.
- Tap **Enter**. Some applications give this key another name, such as **Send**.
- {if:tablet}Tap **Tab** on the onscreen keyboard to move to the next field.
- Make the [back gesture](/basics/go-up-one-level-in-an-app-back-gesture) to leave the screen.

See also [Work with the Text Assist feature](/text-assist/work-with-the-text-assist-feature).
