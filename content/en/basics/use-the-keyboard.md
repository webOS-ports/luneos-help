What the keys do depends on your keyboard. On most of them:

- **Shift:** Hold it for a capital letter.
- **Alt:** Enters the numbers and symbols printed on the keys ([learn how](/basics/enter-alternate-keyboard-characters)).
- **Sym:** Enters a second set of characters, if your keyboard has it.
- **Enter:** Starts a new line, or accepts what you entered.

In Card view, just start typing to open [Just Type](/just-type/open-an-application).

To use the onscreen keyboard as well, open the [system menu](/basics/open-the-connection-menu) and tap **Show On-screen Keyboard**.
