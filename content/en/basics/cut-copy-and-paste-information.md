This works in text fields in any application.

**To cut or copy**

1. Select the text ([learn how](/basics/select-text)).
2. Tap the selected text, then tap **Cut** or **Copy**.

**To paste**

1. Tap and hold in the text field where you want the text.
2. Tap **Paste**.

**Paste** appears only when there is copied text. Text that you can't edit, such as most of a web page, may have its own selection tools.
