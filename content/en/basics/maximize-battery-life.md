Battery life depends on how you use your device. These things help:

- Charge your device whenever you can.
- Set the screen to turn off sooner ([learn how](/screen-and-lock/set-the-interval-for-turning-the-screen-off-automatically)).
- {if:brightness}Lower the screen brightness ([learn how](/screen-and-lock/adjust-the-screen-brightness)).
- Keep the device away from direct sunlight and other heat.
- Turn on airplane mode if you're somewhere with no coverage ([learn how](/basics/turn-all-wireless-services-on-off-airplane-mode)).
- Get email less often ([learn how](/email/enter-advanced-account-settings)).
- Use Wi-Fi for system updates ([learn how](/system-updates/update-the-system-software-from-a-notification)).
- Shut down the device if you won't use it for a long time ([learn how](/basics/shut-down-your-device)).
