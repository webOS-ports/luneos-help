On the onscreen keyboard, do one of the following:

- {if:tablet}To enter a symbol shown on a number key: Tap **Shift**, and then the key.
- {if:tablet}To enter a symbol from the symbol keyboard: Tap **+=[]**, and then a symbol. Tap **ABC** to go back.
- {if:!tablet}To enter a number or symbol: Tap **123**, and then the key. Tap **ABC** to go back.
- To enter an accented letter or another alternate character: Tap and hold a key, and then tap a character in the row that appears.

Not all keys have alternate characters.
