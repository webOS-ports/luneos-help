- Tap **Shift**, and then a letter.
- To turn on Caps Lock: Tap **Shift** twice, or tap and hold it.
- To turn off Caps Lock: Tap **Shift**.
- {if:keyboard}On a physical keyboard, hold **Shift** while you press a letter.
