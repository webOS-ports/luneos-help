Numbers and symbols are printed on the keys of your keyboard.

- To enter one character: Press **Alt**, and then press the key.
- To enter several: Press **Alt** twice to lock it. Press **Alt** again to unlock it.

Some keyboards have a **Sym** key too, for a second set of characters. It works the same way. On some keyboards you hold **Alt** or **Sym** down instead, because the keyboard itself handles those keys.
