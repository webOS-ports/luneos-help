1. Tap and hold the text in a text field.
2. Tap **Select** to select the word, or **Select All** to select all the text in the field.

Tap the selected text to see **Cut** and **Copy**.

Tap anywhere else to clear the selection.

See also [Cut, copy, and paste information](/basics/cut-copy-and-paste-information).
