Just Type searches your applications, contacts and other content, and the web, from one field.

1. [Display Card view](/basics/display-card-view-minimize-an-application) and tap the **Just type...** field.
2. Type your search term. Results appear as you type, under the tabs **All**, **Contacts**, **Content** and **Actions**.
3. Tap a result.

See the articles under [Just Type](/just-type/open-an-application) for each kind of result.
