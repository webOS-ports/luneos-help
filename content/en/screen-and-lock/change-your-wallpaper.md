1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Appearance**.
2. Under **Wallpaper**, tap **Change Wallpaper**.
3. Tap a picture.

To use your own picture, first copy it to a folder named **wallpapers** on your device ([learn how](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer)). Your device scales and crops the picture to fit the screen.
