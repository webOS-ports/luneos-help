1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Screen & Lock**.
2. Under **Secure Unlock**, tap **Change PIN**, **Change Password**, or **Change Pattern**.
3. Enter your current PIN or password > **Continue**, or draw your current pattern.
4. Enter the new PIN or password twice > **Save**, or draw the new pattern twice.
