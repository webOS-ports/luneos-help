1. In LuneOS Explorer, tap **Select**, or tap and hold a file. Then tap more files to add them. **Select All** takes everything in the folder.
2. Do one of the following:
   - To copy or move: Tap **Copy** or **Cut**, go to the other folder, and tap **Paste**.
   - To delete: Tap **Delete** and confirm. This can't be undone.
   - To rename: Select one file, tap **More** > **Rename**.
   - To see size and contents: Tap **More** > **Details**.
3. Tap **Done** to leave selecting.

If a file of that name is already there, tap **Replace**, **Skip These** or **Keep Both**.

To make a folder, tap **New Folder**, enter a name and tap **Create**.
