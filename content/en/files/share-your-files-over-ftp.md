Other computers on your network can open your device's storage.

1. In LuneOS Explorer, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Share Over FTP**.
2. Enter a **Name**, **Password** and **Port**. Turn on **Read-only** if others shouldn't change anything.
3. Tap **Start Sharing**. The address to use on the other computer is shown.

Sharing goes on when you close the app, and keeps your device awake. To stop, open the same dialog and tap **Stop Sharing**.
