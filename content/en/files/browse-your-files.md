LuneOS Explorer is the file manager. You find it in the Launcher, or by searching for "Files".

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) LuneOS Explorer**.
2. Tap a folder to open it. The path at the top shows where you are, and you can tap any part of it. The buttons beside it go back, forward and up.

   On a tablet the folders are on the left. On a phone, tap **Folders** to see them, and **Files** to go back.
3. Tap a file to open it in the app that reads it. Pictures and text files open in LuneOS Explorer itself.

**Also useful**

- **Sort:** Sort by name, date, type or size.
- **Icons:** Switch between a list and icons.
- **Search:** Narrow the folder as you type. To look deeper, tap **Search Subfolders**.
- To see hidden files: Open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Show Hidden Files**.
- To keep a folder at the top of the tree: Open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Add to Favorites**.
