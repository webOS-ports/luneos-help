LuneOS Explorer can open folders on FTP, SFTP, Windows (SMB) and WebDAV servers.

1. In LuneOS Explorer, tap the empty end of the path at the top.
2. Enter an address such as sftp://server, smb://server or davs://server/path, and tap **Go**.
3. If asked, enter your name and password and tap **Connect**.

Copy, Cut, Paste, Delete, Rename and New Folder work here too. To keep the server in the folder tree, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Add to Favorites**.
