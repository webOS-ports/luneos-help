LuneOS Explorer can reach the whole system, but it protects files outside your internal storage. Leave this alone unless you know what you are doing: a mistake can stop your device from starting.

1. In LuneOS Explorer, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Allow System Changes**.
2. Read the warning and tap **Allow**.

This stays on until you turn it off or close the app.
