**To extract:** Tap an archive such as a .zip, .7z, .rar or .tar file, then tap **Extract to** a folder or **Extract Here**. If it has a password, enter it.

**To compress:**

1. Select the files.
2. Tap **More** > **Compress**.
3. Enter a name. Its ending, .zip, .7z or .tar, sets the kind of archive.
