1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Accounts** > **Add an Account**.
2. Scroll to the account you want, or type its name in **Search accounts**, and tap it.
3. Sign in. Depending on the account, enter your username or email address and password and tap **Sign In**, or sign in on the provider's own page.
4. If the account offers a choice of apps, turn each one on or off under **USE ACCOUNT WITH**, and then tap **Create Account**.
