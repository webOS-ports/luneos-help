1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Authenticator** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Change the order**.
2. Tap **Up** or **Down** on an account to move it.
3. Tap **Done**.

You need at least two accounts to do this.
