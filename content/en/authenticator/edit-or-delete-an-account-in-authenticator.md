1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Authenticator**.
2. Tap and hold the account.
3. Do one of the following:
   - To change it: Tap **Edit** > make your changes > **Save**. Leave **Key** empty to keep the key you have.
   - To delete it: Tap **Delete** > confirm.

**Before you delete an account,** turn off two-step verification on the site, or keep the site's recovery codes. Without the account, you can't make codes for that site.
