1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Date & Time**.
2. Set any of the following:

   **Clock:** Under **Time Format**, tap **12 hour** or **24 hour**.

   **Network Time:** Turn this on to have your device get the time from the network. If you turn it off, set the **Date** and **Time** under **Set Date & Time**.

   {if:modem}**Network Time Zone:** Turn this on to have your device get the time zone from the network. Turn it off to choose the time zone yourself.

   **Time Zone:** Tap the current time zone > search for a country or city > tap the location.
