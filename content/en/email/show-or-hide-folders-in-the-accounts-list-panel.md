1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email**.
2. Tap **Favorites** or an account name to show its folders > tap the folder name you want to open.
3. (Optional) **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **About This Folder** to view information about the folder.
4. Tap **Favorites** or the account name again to hide the folders.

The accounts list contains expandable sections for Favorites and each of your email accounts. Favorites gives you quick access to the folders you choose. It can also contain All Inboxes, a merged view of all your inboxes, and All Flagged, which displays all your flagged messages in a single folder.
