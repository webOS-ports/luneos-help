1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email**.
2. If the folders for the account you want are hidden, tap the account name to display the folders.
3. Tap the folder you want to check messages for. If synchronization doesn't start, tap the sync icon at the bottom of the message list.
4. Tap a message to open it.
5. (Optional) Do any of the following:
   - To send an email to an email address: Tap the email address > enter your message > **Send**.
   - To go to a web address: Tap it.
   - To copy a link: Tap and hold the link > **Copy URL**.

The Email application synchronizes messages anytime you open a mail folder. It also synchronizes messages on a schedule that you can set ([**learn how**](/email/enter-advanced-account-settings)).
