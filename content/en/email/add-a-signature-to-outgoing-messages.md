1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts**.
2. Tap the account > tap the **Signature** field > enter signature text.
3. Tap **Done** > **Done**.

A signature includes information you want to add to the closing of all your outgoing messages, for example, your name and phone number. You can use a different signature for each email account.
