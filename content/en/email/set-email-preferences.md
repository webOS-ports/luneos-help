These settings apply to all your email accounts.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts**.
2. Set any of the following:

   - **Smart Folders:** Set whether to include All Inboxes and All Flagged as favorites at the top of the accounts list.
   - **Delete Confirmation:** Tap to turn this setting On/Off. When Delete Confirmation is on, you are asked to confirm deletion of email messages.
   - **Accounts:** Tap an account name to enter advanced settings ([**learn how**](/email/enter-advanced-account-settings)).
   - **Add Account:** [Learn how](/email/set-up-email).
   - **Default Account:** Tap the account shown and then tap the account you want to set as the new default. The default account is used to send a message when you create a new message in All Inboxes.
3. Tap **Done**.
