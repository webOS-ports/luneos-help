Do one of the following:

- In Card view, tap **Just type...** > type a few characters of a name, email address, or subject of an email. Tap **Email** > scroll to find the email you want > tap to open it.
- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** > open the folder containing the message. Tap **Search** above the message list > start typing a name, email address, or subject line of a message you're looking for. Matching results appear as you type. Keep entering characters to narrow the list.

You can search for messages in any email folder. Incoming messages must be fully downloaded to your device to show up in search results.
