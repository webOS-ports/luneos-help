1. While composing a message, tap the paper clip next to **Send**.
2. Tap the kind of file you want, and browse to the file.
3. Tap the file name or thumbnail > **OK**.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 to attach other files.

To remove an attachment you added: Throw the attachment off the side of the screen > tap **Delete**.
