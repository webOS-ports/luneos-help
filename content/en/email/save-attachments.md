1. Open the email containing the attachment.
2. Do one of the following:
   - If only one file is attached: Tap and hold the attachment file name > tap **Save Attachment**.
   - If there is more than one attachment and you want to save one of them: Tap the attachments > tap and hold the attachment you want to save > tap **Save Attachment**.
   - If there is more than one attachment and you want to save all of them: Tap the attachments > tap and hold one of them > tap **Save All Attachments**.
