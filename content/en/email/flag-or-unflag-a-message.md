Do one of the following:

- To flag or unflag a single message: Open a message > tap the flag icon at the top of the message.
- To flag more than one message: Tap the multi-select icon at the bottom of the message list > tap the messages you want to flag > tap the flag icon.

When you flag a message, a flag appears beside the message in the message list. You might flag a message to highlight it as something that you need to return to.
