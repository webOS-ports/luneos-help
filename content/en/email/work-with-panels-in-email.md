<!-- ui:phone -->

Email has three panels: accounts, messages, and the open message. Your phone shows one panel at a time. To go back a panel, make the back gesture ([**learn how**](/basics/go-up-one-level-in-an-app-back-gesture)).

<!-- ui:tablet -->

Email has three panels: accounts, messages, and the open message. In landscape, you see several panels at once. You can resize or hide these panels ([**learn how**](/basics/resize-panels-display-or-hide-levels-in-an-app)).
