1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email**.
2. **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts** > account name.
3. Tap **Change login settings** > tap fields to change information.

When you change your password for an online email account, remember to make the change in the account settings on your device.
