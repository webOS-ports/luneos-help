1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** > open a folder.
2. Tap the multi-select icon at the bottom of the message list.
3. Tap each message you want. The top of the list shows how many you've selected.
4. Tap one of the icons at the bottom of the list:
   - Move icon: Tap a folder to move the messages to.
   - Trash icon: Tap **Delete** to confirm.
   - Flag icon: Flag the messages. If they're all flagged already, this removes the flags.

To leave without changing anything, tap **Cancel**.

In All Inboxes, you can move messages only if they're all from the same account.
