1. Do one of the following in a message you've received:
   - To add the sender: Tap the sender's name at the top of the message.
   - To add someone from the To or Cc field: Tap the **To** line > tap a name in the list of recipients.
2. Tap one of the following:
   - **Add New Contact:** Create a new contact for the name or address.
   - **Add To Existing:** Tap the contact you want to add the name or address to.
