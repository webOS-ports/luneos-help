1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email**.
2. If you have not set up an email account, you need to do so first ([**learn how**](/email/set-up-email)).
3. Tap the new message icon at the bottom of the message list.
4. (Optional) Tap the account next to **from:** to change the email account you are using to send the message.
5. Tap the **To:** field, and enter a contact name or email address > tap the address. For a recipient who is not a contact, enter the full email address.
6. (Optional) Tap **To:** to open the **CC:** and **BCC:** fields > tap either field and enter an address.
7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 to enter additional recipients.
8. Tap the **Subject:** field > enter the subject > tap below it and enter the body text.
9. (Optional) To set the priority for the message: Tap the options button to the left of the paper clip > **Mark as High Priority**.
10. (Optional) To discard a message: Tap the options button > **Discard Message**.
11. Tap **Send**.

If you have set up an email account, you can also start writing an email from Card view. Tap **Just type...**, and start typing a message. Under **Quick Actions**, tap **New Email** (tap **More...** if you don't see it). Email opens at a new message, with the text you entered in the body of the message.
