Do one of the following:

- In the message list: Throw the message off the side of the screen.
- In an open message: Tap the trash icon at the bottom of the message.
- To delete more than one message at once: See [**Select several messages at once**](/email/select-several-messages-at-once).

If Delete Confirmation is on, tap **Delete** to confirm ([**learn how**](/email/set-email-preferences)).

Deleting a message moves it to your deleted items folder. Depending on your email account, this folder is called Deleted Items, Deleted, Trash, or something else. If you accidentally delete a message, you can move it back to the original folder ([**learn how**](/email/move-a-message-to-another-folder)).

To delete all messages from the deleted items folder: Open the folder > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Empty Trash**.
