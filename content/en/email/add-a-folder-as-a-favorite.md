1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email**.
2. If the folder you want is not displayed, tap the email account name to display its folders.
3. Tap the star to the right of the folder name.

Favorites appear at the top of the accounts list.

To remove the folder from favorites: Repeat steps 1 and 2, and tap the star again.
