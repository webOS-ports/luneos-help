The settings you select here apply to individual email accounts. You can apply different settings to each email account.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts** > tap an account under **Accounts**.
2. Next to **Show Icon**, tap **On** or **Off**.
3. Tap **Alert** > tap any of the following:

   **Vibrate:** The device vibrates with no other sound.

   **System Sound:** The system sound plays.

   **Ringtone** > **Select Ringtone:** Tap a ringtone.

   **Mute:** No sound plays and the device does not vibrate.
4. Tap **Done** > **Done**.
