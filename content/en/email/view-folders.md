1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Email** to see the accounts list.
2. Do any of the following:

   - View all folders for an account: Tap the account name.
   - View the contents of a folder: Tap the folder name.
   - View folder information: Open the folder > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **About This Folder**.
