This works for events that have attendees, such as meetings from an online calendar.

1. Tap the event name > tap the attendees line.
2. Tap **Contact Attendees**.

   This opens an email message with the attendees' addresses added to the To field. The default message text is "Running late. On my way...". You can edit the text before sending the message.
3. Tap **Send**.
