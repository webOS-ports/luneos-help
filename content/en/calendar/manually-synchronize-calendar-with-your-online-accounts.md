**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calendar** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Sync Now**.

If you want to get calendar events from an online account on your device immediately, or upload events from your device to the online account (if the account allows it), you can do a manual sync. This synchronizes Calendar with all the accounts you have set up on your device.
