1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calendar**.
2. Do one of the following:
   - To use Calendar without an online account: Tap **LuneOS**. You can now start creating events on your device.
   - To set up an online account to sync: Tap the account type and go to step 3.
   - To use accounts you have already set up in another app: Tap **Go**. To add another account, tap **Add an Account** instead and tap the account type.
3. Enter the account information > **Sign In**.
4. Depending on the account, you may be presented with options for which apps should download data from that account. For each app, tap **On** or **Off** > **Create Account**.
