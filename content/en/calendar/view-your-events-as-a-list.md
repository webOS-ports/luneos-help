Agenda shows your events as a list instead of on a time grid.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calendar**.
2. In Day view or Week view, tap **Agenda**.

   Each event shows its time, name, and location. In Day view, the list also shows how much free time you have between events.
3. To go back to the time grid, tap **Agenda** again.

To open Calendar in this view every time: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Default Launch View** > **Day Agenda** or **Week Agenda**. To open Calendar in the view you used last, select **Last Used**.

See Also [**Change the Calendar display**](/calendar/change-the-calendar-display)
