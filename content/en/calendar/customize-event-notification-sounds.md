1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calendar** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts**.
2. Tap the field under **Event Reminders** > tap any of the following:

   **Vibrate:** The device vibrates.

   **System Sound:** The system sound plays.

   **Mute:** No sound plays and the device does not vibrate.
3. Tap **Done**.

The settings you apply here apply to all of your calendars.
