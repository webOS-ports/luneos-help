1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calendar**.
2. Do any of the following:
   - To see different calendar views: Tap the day, week, or month icon at the bottom of the screen.
   - To see a list of events instead of a time grid: In Day or Week view, tap **Agenda** ([**learn how**](/calendar/view-your-events-as-a-list)).
   - To hide or show one calendar: Tap the calendar name at the top of the screen. If you have many calendars, you can also tap **Hide All** or **Show All**.
   - To change the color of a calendar: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts**. Under **Calendar Colors**, tap the calendar > tap a new color > **Done**.
   - To choose the view Calendar opens in: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Default Launch View**.
