1. To create a new event, tap **New event**. To change an event you already created, tap the event name > **Edit**.
2. Under **Repeat**, tap **No Repeat** > **Daily**, **Weekdays**, **Weekly**, or **Custom**. If you tap **Custom**, define your own repeat interval and end date.
3. Tap **Done**.
