- Next or previous day: In Day view, swipe left or right on the screen.
- Next or previous week: In Week view, swipe left or right on the screen.
- Next or previous month: In Month view, swipe left or right on the screen.
- To go to a specific day from Week or Month view: Tap the day.
- To go to a specific date: Tap **Jump to...** at the bottom of the screen > tap the month, day, and year > **Go To Date**.
- Go to today: Tap **Jump to...** > **Show Today**.
