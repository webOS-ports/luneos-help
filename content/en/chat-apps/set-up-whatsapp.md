You link your device to WhatsApp on another phone, so you need that phone with you.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Add An Account** > tap **WhatsApp**.
2. Under **PHONE NUMBER**, enter your number in international format, such as +15551234567. If you like, enter a **DISPLAY NAME**.
3. Tap **Sign In**. A QR code appears, and sometimes a code to type in.
4. On your other phone, open WhatsApp > **Settings** > **Linked Devices** > **Link a Device**. Do one of the following:
   - Scan the QR code.
   - Tap **Link with phone number instead** and enter the code from your device.

Each code lasts a few seconds and refreshes by itself. If yours expires, tap **Get a new code**.
