1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Add An Account** > tap **Telegram**.
2. Under **PHONE NUMBER**, enter your number in international format, such as +15551234567. If you like, enter a **DISPLAY NAME**.
3. Tap **Sign In**. Telegram sends a login code to your Telegram app or by text message.
4. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the **Telegram** chat > reply with the code.
5. If you use a two-step password with Telegram, reply with that too.
