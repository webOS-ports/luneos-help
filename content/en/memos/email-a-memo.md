1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Memos** > tap the memo.
2. Tap the envelope icon at the top of the memo.

   Email opens a new message containing the memo text. The memo title becomes the subject. If the memo has no title, the subject is "Just a quick memo".
3. Enter the email address. You can edit the text before sending the message.
4. Tap **Send**.
