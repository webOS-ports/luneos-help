1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Memos** > tap the memo you want to edit.
2. Tap the memo text to position the cursor (**[learn how](/basics/place-the-cursor-in-a-text-field)**) > edit the text. To change the title, tap it at the top of the memo.

Your changes are saved as you type.
