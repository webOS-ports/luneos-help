1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Memos**.
2. Tap **+** at the bottom of the memo list > enter the memo text.
3. (Optional) Tap **Title...** at the top of the memo to give it a title. If you don't, the memo uses the start of its text as the title ([**learn how**](/memos/give-a-memo-a-title)).

Memos are saved as you type.

To create a memo using Just Type: In Card view, tap **Just type...** > start typing your memo. Under **Quick Actions**, tap **New Memo** (tap **More...** if you don't see it). Memos opens at a new memo, with the text you entered in the body of the memo.
