1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tasks**.
2. Tap the list containing the tasks > **•••** at the bottom of the list, or **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Set due date for all...**.
3. Tap the month, day, and year > **Set due date**.

   To remove a previously assigned due date, tap **No due date**.
