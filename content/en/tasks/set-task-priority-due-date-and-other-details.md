1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tasks**.
2. Tap the list containing the task > tap the arrow or due date to the right of the task name.
3. Set any of the following:

   **Priority:** High priority tasks appear in red, and low priority tasks appear in gray.

   **List:** Assign the task to a different list.

   **Due:** Tap the current due date, and tap **No due date**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **In one week**, **In one month**, or **Other...**. A notification appears when a task is due. Tap the notification to open the task.

   **Notes:** Tap **Notes...** > enter the note.

Changes are saved as you make them.
