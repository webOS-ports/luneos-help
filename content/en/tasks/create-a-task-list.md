A task list is a group of related to-do items.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tasks**.
2. Tap the new list icon at the bottom of the lists > enter the name in the **List name** field > press **Enter**.

   Enter also starts your first task in the new list ([**learn how**](/tasks/create-a-task)).
