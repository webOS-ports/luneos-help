Do one of the following:

- To delete a task: Throw the task off the side of the screen > **Delete**. Or open its details > **Delete Task** > **Delete**.
- To delete a task list: Throw the list off the side of the screen > **Delete**. Or open the list, tap **•••** at the bottom of the list > **Delete list** > **Delete**.

You can delete only lists you created on the device.
