1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tasks**.
2. Tap the list containing the tasks > **•••** at the bottom of the list, or **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)**.
3. Do one of the following:
   - To hide completed tasks: Tap **Hide Completed**.
   - To delete completed tasks: Tap **Delete completed tasks** > **Delete Completed**.
