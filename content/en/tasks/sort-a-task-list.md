You can sort a task list to show tasks in the order you have determined, or by due date or priority.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tasks**.
2. Tap the list you want to sort.
3. Tap **My Order**, **Due Date**, or **Priority** at the top of the list > tap the sort option you want.
4. To manually sort tasks: Choose **My Order** > tap and hold a task, wait for the visual cue, and then drag it to a new location in the list.

See Also [Check off a task](/tasks/check-off-a-task "Check off a task")
