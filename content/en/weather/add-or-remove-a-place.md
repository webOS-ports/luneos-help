1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Weather**. Swipe left until you see **Menu**.
2. Do one of the following:
   - To add a place: Tap **Add Place** > enter a name in **City or place** > **Search** > tap the place. Weather shows its forecast.
   - To switch to another place: Tap it under **Places**.
   - To remove a place: Tap **×** next to it.

**Current Location** is always in the list. It uses your device's location, and you can't remove it. If Weather can't find you, turn on location services ([**learn how**](/location-services/turn-location-service-providers-on-off)), or add a place.
