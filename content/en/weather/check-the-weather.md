You need an internet connection to get a new forecast.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Weather**.

   The top of the screen shows the temperature, the place, and the current conditions. **Currently** shows what to expect in **Your Hour**, **Your Day**, and **Your Week**, and more details under **Right Now**.
2. Swipe left to see **Hourly**, and again to see **Daily**. Swipe left once more for **Menu**, where you'll find your places and settings.

To go back to **Currently**, use the back gesture ([**learn how**](/basics/go-up-one-level-in-an-app-back-gesture)).

To update the forecast, tap the weather icon at the top of the screen. Weather also updates by itself when you come back to it after 30 minutes.

Without a connection, Weather shows the last forecast it received, and how old it is.

The first time you open Weather, it shows you help boxes. Tap a box to close it. To see them again, go to **Menu** > **Show Help Boxes**.
