1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Weather**. Swipe left until you see **Menu**.
2. Under **Settings**, do any of the following. Changes apply as soon as you make them.
   - **Units:** Select **Imperial** or **Metric**, or **Auto** to use °F for places in the US and °C elsewhere.
   - **Time:** Select **12 hour** or **24 hour**, or **Auto** to follow **Date & Time** ([**learn how**](/date-and-time/use-date-and-time)).
   - **Icon Pack:** Select **Color** or **White** weather icons.
   - **Widget:** See [**Show the weather in the notification area**](/weather/show-the-weather-in-the-notification-area).
