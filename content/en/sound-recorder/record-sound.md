1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Sound Recorder**.
2. Tap the red button.
3. To pause, tap the pause button. Tap it again to go on.
4. To finish, tap the stop button.

Recording goes on if you close the card. Pause and Stop are also in the notification area.

If your device has a privacy switch for the microphone, switch it on first.

Recordings are saved in the Recordings folder of your internal storage, so you can copy them to a computer.
