1. In Sound Recorder, tap and hold a recording, or tap its **…** button.
2. Tap **Rename**, **Send by Email**, **Details** or **Delete**.

You can also swipe a recording away, then tap **Delete** or **Cancel**. A deleted recording can't be restored.
