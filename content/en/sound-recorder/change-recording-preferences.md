1. In Sound Recorder, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Preferences**.
2. Change any of these:
   - **Format:** MP3 is small and plays everywhere. Opus is the smallest. FLAC and WAV keep every detail. Your device offers the formats it can record.
   - **Quality:** **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Very high**.
   - **Channels:** **Stereo** or **Mono**.
   - **Keep screen on while recording**.
   - **Input** and **Input level:** The microphone and how sensitive it is.

Under **Storage** you see the folder and the free space.
