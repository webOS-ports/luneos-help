1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Sound Recorder**.
2. Tap a recording. Its player opens in the list.
3. Use the controls: drag to move, **−10 s**, **Play** or **Pause**, **+10 s**.

With six or more recordings, a **Search** field appears.
