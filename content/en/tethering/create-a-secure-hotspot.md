1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tethering**.
2. Under **Wi-Fi Hotspot**, enter a **Name** for your hotspot.
3. Enter a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
4. Turn on **Share over Wi-Fi**.
5. On the other device, such as your laptop, use its Wi-Fi settings to join the hotspot and enter the password.
