1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Tethering**.
2. Turn on one of the following. Each appears only if your device supports it:
   - **Share over Wi-Fi**: Set up the hotspot first ([learn how](/tethering/create-a-secure-hotspot)).
   - **Share over USB**
   - **Share over Bluetooth**
3. On the other device, connect to your device.
4. When you finish, turn the switch off to save battery power.

For more about the USB connection, see [Connect to a computer with USB](/network/connect-to-a-computer-with-usb).
