**Copy files**

1. Connect your device to the computer with a USB cable.
2. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) USB** > **Connect as media device**. The button works only while a computer is connected.

**Cable** and **Mode** show what's happening. **Mode** shows **Media transfer** while the computer can see your files, and **Charging** when it can't.

Your device stops sharing files when the computer ejects it or you unplug the cable. For more, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).

**Share your internet connection**

Under **Also over the cable**, tap **USB Tethering**. Then see [Use tethering](/tethering/use-tethering).

**Use developer tools**

Under **Also over the cable**, tap **USB Debugging**. This opens Developer Mode.
