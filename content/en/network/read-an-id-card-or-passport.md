NFC isn't installed on every device. If there's no **NFC** app, your device doesn't offer it.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) NFC** > **ID / Passport**. Make sure NFC is on.
2. Enter the six-digit CAN printed on the document.
3. Tap **Read** and hold the document steady against your device.

You see the **Name**, **Document number**, **Nationality**, **Date of birth**, and **Date of expiry**, and the photo if there is one.
