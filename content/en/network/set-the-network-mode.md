1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Network Settings**.
2. Under **Mobile Data**, tap **Network mode**.
3. Tap **Automatic**, **2G (GSM)**, **3G (UMTS)**, or **4G (LTE)**.

**Network mode** appears only if your device can change it.
