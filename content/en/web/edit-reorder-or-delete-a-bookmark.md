<!-- ui:phone -->

1. Tap the Menu button at the bottom right > **Password Manager**. In the drawer that opens, tap the Bookmarks tab.

2. Do any of the following:
   - To edit: Tap the info button at the right of the bookmark > change the title or URL > **Save**.
   - To delete: Swipe the bookmark off the side of the screen and confirm.

To reorder bookmarks, go to the start page of a new tab and drag a thumbnail. To remove one there, press and hold it > **Remove Bookmark**.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

1. Tap the Bookmarks button in the toolbar.

2. Do any of the following:
   - To edit: Tap the info button at the right of the bookmark > change the title or URL > **Save**.
   - To delete: Swipe the bookmark off the side of the screen and confirm.

To reorder bookmarks, go to the start page of a new tab and drag a thumbnail. To remove one there, press and hold it > **Remove Bookmark**.
