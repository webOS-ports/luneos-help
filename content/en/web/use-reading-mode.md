Reading Mode shows a page's article by itself, without menus and ads.

1. Open a page with an article.
2. In Atlas Web, open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) and tap **Reading Mode**. On a phone, the Menu button is at the bottom right.
3. To go back to the page, tap **Done**.

On some pages there is no article to show. A cookie or privacy screen can also get in the way: accept it on the page first.
