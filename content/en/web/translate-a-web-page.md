Atlas Web translates pages with Google Translate, into your device's language. The page's address goes to Google.

**To turn this on:**

1. In Atlas Web, open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) and tap **Preferences**. On a phone, the Menu button is at the bottom right.
2. Turn on **Offer to translate pages**. Tap **Done**.

**To translate a page:** Tap the translate button in the address bar.
