**To save a password:** Sign in on a website. Atlas Web asks whether to save it. Tap **Save**.

The next time you tap the sign-in field on that site, Atlas Web fills it in. If you have saved more than one login for the site, tap one in **Choose a login**.

**To see or change saved passwords:**

1. In Atlas Web, open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) and tap **Password Manager**. On a phone, the Menu button is at the bottom right.
2. Tap a site. To find one, type in **Search**.
3. Tap **Show password**, or change the details and save. To remove the login, tap **Delete password**.

You can also swipe a site off the side of the screen to delete it.
