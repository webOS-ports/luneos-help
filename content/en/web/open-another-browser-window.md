<!-- ui:phone -->

Atlas Web opens more pages as tabs in one card.

- To open a tab: Tap the button with the number of tabs at the bottom of the screen > **New Tab**.
- To switch: Tap that button again > tap a tab. Tap **Done** to go back without switching.
- To open a private tab, whose history is not kept: Tap the Menu button > **New Private Card**.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

Atlas Web opens more pages as tabs in one card.

- To open a tab: Tap the New Card button in the toolbar. With two or more tabs, a row of tabs appears at the top, where **+** opens another.
- To switch: Tap a tab in the row. Tap the **x** on a tab to close it.
- To open a private tab, whose history is not kept: Open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) > **New Private Card**.
