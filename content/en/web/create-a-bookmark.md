<!-- ui:phone -->

1. Open the page you want to bookmark.
2. Tap the address at the top of the screen.
3. Tap the star beside the address.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

1. Open the page you want to bookmark.
2. Do one of the following:
   - Tap the star at the end of the address bar.
   - Tap the Share button > **Add Bookmark**.
   - Tap the Bookmarks button, then tap **+** at the bottom of the Bookmarks tab.
