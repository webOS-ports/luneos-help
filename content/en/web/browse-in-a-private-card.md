A private card doesn't keep your history, or the cookies and cache of what you visit.

1. In Atlas Web, open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) and tap **New Private Card**. On a phone, the Menu button is at the bottom right.
2. Go to a website in the new card.

To end it, close the card.
