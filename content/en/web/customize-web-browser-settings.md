<!-- ui:phone -->

1. In Atlas Web, tap the Menu button at the bottom right > **Preferences**.

2. Set any of the following:

   **Default Web Search Engine:** Choose the search engine for the address bar.

   **Block Popups:** Stops websites from opening new windows.

   **Accept Cookies:** Lets websites store cookies.

   **Enable JavaScript:** Turning this off may stop some websites from working.

   **Offer to translate pages:** Shows a translate button in the address bar.

   **Names and Passwords:** Offers to remember what you enter on websites.

   **Clear Bookmarks**, **Clear History**, **Clear Cookies**, **Clear Cache:** Each asks you to confirm before it deletes.
3. Tap **Done**.
