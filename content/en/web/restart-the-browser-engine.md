If pages stop loading or Atlas Web stops responding:

1. In Atlas Web, open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) and tap **Restart Browser Engine**. On a phone, the Menu button is at the bottom right.
2. Wait a moment while the engine starts again.
