<!-- ui:phone -->

Do one of the following:

- On the start page of a new tab: Tap a thumbnail.
- Tap the address at the top of the screen and type part of the bookmark's name, then tap it in the list.
- Tap **Just type...**, type part of the name > **Bookmarks & History** > tap the bookmark.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

Do one of the following:

- On the start page of a new tab: Tap a thumbnail.
- Tap the Bookmarks button in the toolbar > tap the bookmark.
- Tap the address bar and type part of the bookmark's name, then tap it in the list.
- Tap **Just type...**, type part of the name > **Bookmarks & History** > tap the bookmark.
