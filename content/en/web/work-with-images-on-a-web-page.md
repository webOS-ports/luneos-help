To copy an image's address: Press and hold the image > **Copy Image URL**.

The same menu also lists **Save Image**, **Set Wallpaper**, **Share Image** and **Drag**, but these do nothing in this version of Atlas Web.

If an image is a link to a file, you can press and hold it > **Download Link**.
