<!-- ui:phone -->

- To go back or forward: Tap the arrows at the bottom of the screen. With nowhere to go back to, back closes the tab.
- To reload or stop: Tap the button at the end of the address at the top.
- To see or edit the address: Tap it.

- To scroll: Drag up or down.
- To zoom: Pinch out or in.
- To find text on the page: Open the menu > **Find on Page**.
- To read without clutter: Open the menu > **Reading Mode**.
- To share the address: Press and hold an empty part of the page > **Share Link**.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

- To go back or forward: Tap the arrows at the left of the toolbar. Press and hold one to pick from the pages you visited.
- To reload or stop: Tap the button at the end of the address bar.

- To scroll: Drag up or down.
- To zoom: Pinch out or in.
- To find text on the page: Open the menu > **Find on Page**.
- To read without clutter: Open the menu > **Reading Mode**.
- To share the address: Press and hold an empty part of the page > **Share Link**.
