Do one of the following:

- Tap **Just type...**, type the site address > tap **Go to website**.
- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Atlas Web** > tap the address bar > enter the address. If the page appears in the list below, tap it. If not, press Enter.

To stop a page while it loads, tap the cross at the end of the address bar.
