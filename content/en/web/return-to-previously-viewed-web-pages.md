<!-- ui:phone -->

Do one of the following:

- Tap the address bar and type part of the page's name or address, then tap it in the list.
- Tap **Just type...**, type part of the name > **Bookmarks & History** > tap the page.

To see all your history: Tap the Menu button at the bottom right > **Password Manager**. In the drawer, tap the History tab, then tap a page. To delete an entry, swipe it off the side of the screen.

To clear your history: Tap the Menu button > **Preferences** > **Clear History**.

<!-- ui:tablet -->

Do one of the following:

- Tap the address bar and type part of the page's name or address, then tap it in the list.
- Tap **Just type...**, type part of the name > **Bookmarks & History** > tap the page.

To see all your history: Tap the Bookmarks button in the toolbar > tap the History tab, then tap a page. To delete an entry, swipe it off the side of the screen.

To clear your history: Open the [Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu) > **Preferences** > **Clear History**.
