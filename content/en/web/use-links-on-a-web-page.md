- To go to another page: Tap the link.
- To open a link in another tab: Press and hold the link > **Open In New Card**.
- To read a link without clutter: Press and hold the link > **Reading Mode**.
- To share a link: Press and hold the link > **Share Link**.
- To copy a link: Press and hold the link > **Copy URL**.
- To download what a link points to: Press and hold the link > **Download Link**.
