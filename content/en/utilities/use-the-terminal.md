Terminal gives you a command line on your device. It is meant for people who know their way around Linux. A wrong command can damage your system or erase your information.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Terminal**.
2. Type a command and press Enter on the keyboard.

**Keys a phone keyboard does not have**

A bar above the onscreen keyboard offers Ctrl, Esc, arrows, function keys and some ready-made commands. The button at the left of the bar switches between **Scroll**, **Cmds**, **Fx**, **Nano** and **Ctrl**. A key with a dot has more choices: press and hold it.

**Gestures on the terminal**

- Drag one finger: Moves the cursor and goes through earlier commands.
- Drag two fingers: Scrolls back through the output.
- Press and hold: Opens **Copy**, **Paste** and **Select All**.
