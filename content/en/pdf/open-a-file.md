1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) PDF Viewer**.
2. Tap a file. The files you read last are at the top, under **Recent**, with how far you got.
3. To go back to the list, use the back gesture.

To find a file, type its name in **Search**. To sort, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Sort by Name**, **Sort by Date** or **Sort by Size**. If a new file is not listed, tap **Refresh** in the same menu.

If a file is protected, enter its password and tap **Open**.

PDF files also open from LuneOS Explorer, Email and the browser's downloads. To add files from a computer, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).
