1. With the file open, tap the Contents button at the left of the bar at the bottom. If the bar is hidden, tap the page.
2. Tap **Contents**.
3. Tap an entry to jump to that part of the file.

**Contents** lists what the file's author set up. **Pages** shows all pages.
