With a file open, drag up or down to move through the pages. A tap on the page shows or hides the bars.

- To go to the next or previous page: Tap the Next or Previous button in the bar at the bottom.
- To jump to a page: Tap the page number between those buttons > enter the number > **Go**. You can also use the More button > **Go to Page…**.
- To see all pages: Tap the Contents button at the left of the bar > **Pages** > tap a page.
- To find text: Tap the Search button at the top, or the More button > **Find…**.
- To turn the page: Tap the More button > **Rotate Clockwise** or **Rotate Counterclockwise**.
- To read in the dark: Tap the More button > **Night Mode**.

The file opens where you left it next time.
