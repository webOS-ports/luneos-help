1. In PDF Viewer, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)**.
2. Tap one of these:
   - **Night Mode:** Dark pages, for reading in the dark. Tap it again to turn it off.
   - **Rotate Clockwise** or **Rotate Counterclockwise:** Turns the pages.
