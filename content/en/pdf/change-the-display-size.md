With a file open:

- Pinch out to enlarge the page. Pinch in to make it smaller.
- Double-tap the page to zoom in or out.
- Tap the Fit button at the end of the bar at the bottom to switch between fitting the width and fitting the whole page.

If the bar is hidden, tap the page.
