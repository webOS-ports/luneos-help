1. In PDF Viewer, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Find…**.
2. Type in **Find in document** and press Enter.

Use the arrows beside the field to go to the previous or next match, or press Enter again. Tap the cross to close the search.
