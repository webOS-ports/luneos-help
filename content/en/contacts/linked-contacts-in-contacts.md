If the same person is in more than one of your accounts, each account holds a profile of that person. Contacts links profiles together, so you see one contact with all the information.

The button in a contact's header shows how many profiles are linked. Tap it to see them. To link profiles yourself, see [Link a contact](/contacts/link-a-contact). To separate them, see [Unlink a contact](/contacts/unlink-a-contact).
