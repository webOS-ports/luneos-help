1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Contacts** > tap the add button at the lower right of the list.
2. If you have more than one contacts account, choose the account to save the contact in, at the top of the screen.
3. Enter a name. Tap the button next to the name to enter a prefix, first, middle, and last name, or a suffix.
4. Enter any of the other information. Tap a **New** field, such as **New phone number** or **New email address**, to add an entry. Choose a label from the list beside the entry. To remove an entry, tap the **×** next to it.
5. Tap **Create**.

Enter at least a name or a number. If you don't, you see **Type a name or a number first**.
