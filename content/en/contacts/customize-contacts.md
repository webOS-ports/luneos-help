1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Contacts** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Preferences & Accounts**.
2. Under **List Order**, choose **First name**, **Last name**, **Company & first name**, or **Company & last name**.
3. Under **Default account**, choose the account that new contacts are saved in.
4. Tap **Done**.
