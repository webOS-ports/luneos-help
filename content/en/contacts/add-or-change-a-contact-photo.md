You can't add a photo to a contact on your device. A contact's photo comes from the online account the contact syncs with, so change the photo in that account.

To remove a photo:

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Contacts** > tap the contact.
2. Tap the edit button at the lower right.
3. Tap **Remove Photo** > **Save**.
