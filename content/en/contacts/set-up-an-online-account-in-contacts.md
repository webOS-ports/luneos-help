1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Contacts** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Add an account**.
2. Tap the type of account.
3. Enter your sign-in information > follow the steps on the screen ([learn more](/accounts/use-the-accounts-application-to-set-up-an-online-account)).

Contacts in the account appear in Contacts.
