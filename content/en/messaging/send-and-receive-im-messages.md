First set up an IM account ([learn how](/messaging/set-up-an-im-account)).

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > do one of the following:
   - Tap the buddies tab > tap the buddy.
   - Tap **New Conversation** > enter a name in the **To** field > tap the match.
2. Enter your message > tap the send button, or press **Enter**.

Messages you receive appear in the conversation. You can't send files or voice messages yet ([learn more](/messaging/open-pictures-files-and-voice-messages)).
