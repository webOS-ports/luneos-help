The servers tab shows the group chats of your accounts, such as Discord servers and Teams teams. Set up the account first ([learn how](/chat-apps/what-chat-services-can-i-use)).

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the servers tab, at the left of the tabs.
2. Tap a server. To find one, enter its name in **Search servers**.
3. Tap a channel. It opens as a conversation.
4. Enter your message > tap the send button.

To go back to the list of servers, tap **Servers** at the bottom of the screen.

To start a private conversation with someone in a channel, tap their name above their message.
