1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** (upper left) > **Preferences & Accounts** > **Add An Account**.
2. Tap the type of account.
3. Enter your sign-in information ([learn more](/accounts/use-the-accounts-application-to-set-up-an-online-account)).
4. Tap **Done**.

Some services have their own sign-in steps. See [What chat services can I use?](/chat-apps/what-chat-services-can-i-use)
