1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the favorites tab > **Add Favorite**.
2. Choose the contact.

If choosing a contact doesn't work here, make the contact a favorite in Contacts instead ([learn how](/contacts/create-a-favorite)).

If a favorite shows **Tap here to add contact info**, tap it to add a number or address in Contacts.
