1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the conversation.
2. Tap the message, or drag it a short way to the right.
3. Tap an emoji.

To take your reaction back, tap it under the message.

Reactions are for IM conversations. Whether the other person sees yours depends on the service.
