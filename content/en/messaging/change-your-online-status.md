1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the status bar at the top of the screen.
2. Under **ALL ACCOUNTS**, tap **Available**, **Busy**, **Invisible**, or **Offline**. **Invisible** appears only for accounts that support it.

To set the status of one account, tap the status under **INDIVIDUAL ACCOUNTS**.
