1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > **New Conversation**.
2. In the **To** field, enter a number or the name of a contact > tap the match.
3. Enter your message > tap the send button.

You can send to one recipient at a time. To continue a conversation, tap it in the list.
