**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Messaging** > tap the conversation. Then do any of the following:

- Reply: Enter your message > tap the send button.
- React to a message: Tap it > tap an emoji ([learn more](/messaging/react-to-a-message)).
- Work with a message: Tap it > **•••** > **Reply**, **Copy Text**, or **Delete**.
- Delete a message: Drag it to the left > **Delete**.
- Delete the conversation: Tap **Delete Conversation** > **Delete**.
