1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Camera**.
2. Tap the settings button at the top left, or open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences**.
3. Change any of these under **Camera**:
   - **Self-timer:** **Off**, or from **3 seconds** to **1 minute**.
   - **Picture quality:** **Normal**, **High** or **Best**.
   - **Shutter sound**, **Grid**, **Tag photos with location** and **Read QR codes and barcodes**.
4. Under **Picture**, you find what your camera supports: **HDR**, **Zero shutter lag**, **Scene**, **White balance**, **Effect**, **ISO**, **Exposure**, **Focus** and **Flicker reduction**.

Which of the **Picture** settings you see depends on your camera. They appear once the camera has started.

Beside the settings button, small badges show what is on, like **HDR**, **GPS** or the self-timer. Tap a badge to turn it off.
