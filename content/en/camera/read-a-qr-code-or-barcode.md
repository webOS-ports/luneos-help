1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Camera** and point it at the code. A banner appears with what the code says.
2. Tap **Copy** or **Open**, or **Close** to dismiss it.

A code for two-step sign-in shows as **Two-step verification**. To turn off reading codes, open Camera Preferences and turn off **Read QR codes and barcodes**.
