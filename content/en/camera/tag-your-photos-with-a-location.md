1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Camera** and open Camera Preferences ([learn how](/camera/change-camera-preferences)).
2. Turn on **Tag photos with location**. A **GPS** badge appears on the screen.

Your location must be on ([learn how](/location-services/turn-location-service-providers-on-off)). You can also set this in Location Services, with **Geotag Photos**.
