- **Front or back:** Tap the button with the circling arrows at the top right. It appears only if your device has more than one camera.
- **Another lens:** If this side has more than one camera, a button below it shows which is on, like 1/2. Tap it. You can also pick one in Camera Preferences under **Camera**.

You can't switch while recording.
