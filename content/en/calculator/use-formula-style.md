In Formula Style, you enter the whole calculation first, and then see the result.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calculator** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Formula Style**.
2. Enter the calculation, for example `(2+3)*4`.

   | Button | What it does |
   | --- | --- |
   | **√** | Square root. Enter the number, then tap **)**. |
   | **(** and **)** | Group part of a calculation. |
   | **^** | Raise to a power, for example `2^8`. |
   | **x²** | Square the number before it. |
   | **ln** | Natural logarithm. Enter the number, then tap **)**. |
   | **log** | Base-10 logarithm. Enter the number, then tap **)**. |
   | **/** and **\*** | Divide and multiply. |
   | Backspace arrow, beside the calculation | Delete the last character. |
   | **C** | Clear the calculation and the result. |
3. Tap **=**. The result appears above the calculation.

If the calculation can't be worked out, **Invalid Input** appears. Check that every **(** has a matching **)**.

To go back: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Traditional Style**.
