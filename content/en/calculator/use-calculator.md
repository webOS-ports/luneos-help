1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Calculator**.
2. Enter numbers and perform calculations.

   Calculator buttons:

   | Button | What it does |
   | --- | --- |
   | **C** | Clear the current calculation and the display. |
   | Backspace arrow, beside the display | Delete the last digit you entered. |
   | **M+** | Add the displayed number to memory. An **M** appears on the display while a number is in memory. |
   | **MR** | Show the number in memory. |
   | **MC** | Clear the memory. |

To switch style: **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Traditional Style** or **Formula Style**.

In Formula Style, you enter the whole calculation, then tap **=** to see the result. This style adds √, parentheses, **^** (power), **ln**, **log**, and **x²**. If the calculation can't be worked out, **Invalid Input** appears ([**learn how**](/calculator/use-formula-style)).
