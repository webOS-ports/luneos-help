1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Appearance**.
2. Under **Cards**, turn these on or off:

   - **Tap Ripple:** Shows a ripple where you tap the screen.
   - **Stacked Cards:** Lets you [stack cards](/basics/stack-cards).
   - **Gesture Area:** Shows the strip at the bottom of the screen. Turning it off gives that space back to whatever is on the screen.
