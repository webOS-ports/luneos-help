1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Appearance**.
2. Under **Launcher**, do any of the following:

   - **Tab Titles:** Tap and choose **UPPERCASE**, **lowercase**, or **Capitalized Case**.
   - **Indicators:** Tap and choose how many tabs to show at a time: **Default**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, or **All**. With **Default**, the tab row scrolls.
   - **Android Tab:** Turn on to give Android apps their own tab. When it's off, they stay on the **Apps** tab. This is useful only on a device that runs Android apps.
