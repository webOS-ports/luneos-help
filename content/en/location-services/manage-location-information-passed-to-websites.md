1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Location Services**.
2. Under **For Websites**, tap **Location** and choose one:

   **Always Ask:** You are asked each time a website wants your location.

   **Never Share Location:** Websites never get your location.
3. To have everything asked again next time, tap **Clear My Location Data** > **Clear**.
