1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Location Services**.
2. Look under **Application Access**. It lists the applications that have asked for your location, with your answer.
3. To change an answer, tap the application and choose **Allowed**, **Denied** or **Ask Again**. Some applications show **Allowed (system)** or **Allowed (by app)**. Those were set when they were installed.

If the list is empty, no application has asked yet. To change whether applications can use your location at all, see [Manage location information passed to applications](/location-services/manage-location-information-passed-to-applications).
