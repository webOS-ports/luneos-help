1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Location Services**.
2. Under **GPS Status**, turn on **Show My Location**.

You see **Waiting for a fix...** until your device finds its position. It then shows your **Latitude**, **Longitude** and **Accuracy**. GPS works best outdoors.

Turn it off when you are done: GPS uses battery.
