1. While a video plays, tap the screen to show the controls.
2. Do one of the following:
   - To change subtitles: Tap the subtitles button, then tap **Off**, **From file** or a subtitle track. Under **Text size**, tap a size.
   - To change the sound: Tap the audio button and tap a track. Most videos have only one.

Videos remembers your choice for that video.

**To add subtitles to a video:** Copy a SubRip file to the same folder, with the same name as the video. For example, movie.srt or movie.en.srt beside movie.mp4. Videos turns on the subtitles by itself.
