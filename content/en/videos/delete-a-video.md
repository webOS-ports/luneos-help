The Videos app cannot delete files. Use Photos & Videos:

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap the album > tap the video.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the screen.
3. Tap the Delete button > **Delete**.

Pressing and holding a video in the Videos app only offers to play it from the start or take it off Continue Watching.
