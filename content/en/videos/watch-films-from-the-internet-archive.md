The Internet Archive in Videos needs a newer LuneOS build than some devices have.

The Internet Archive (archive.org) has public films, classic TV, cartoons and more, free to watch. There is nothing to sign in to.

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Accounts** > **Add an Account** > **Internet Archive** under **OTHER** > **Add**.
2. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Videos** and tap the **Internet Archive** tab.
3. Tap a film in **Feature Films**, **Classic TV** or **Animation & Cartoons**, or tap a collection such as **Silent Films**, **Film Noir** or **Short Films** to see all of it.

To find a film, type in **Search** under the tabs and tap Enter.

The **Internet Archive** under **MUSIC** in Accounts is a different account: it adds the Archive's music to Music.
