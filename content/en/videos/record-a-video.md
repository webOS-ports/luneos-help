1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Camera**.
2. Tap the photo/video switch at the end of the control bar.
3. Tap the record button. A timer shows how long you have been recording.
4. Tap the stop button.

To turn the video light on or off, tap the flash button before you start. The button appears only if the camera has a light.

To watch the video, see [Watch a video](/videos/watch-a-video).
