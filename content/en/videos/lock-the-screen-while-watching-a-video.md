The lock turns off the controls and every tap. It is useful when you hold the device or carry it while it plays.

**To lock:** Tap the screen to show the controls, then tap the lock at the top right.

**To unlock:**

1. Tap the screen. **Screen locked** appears for a few seconds.
2. Tap it. It changes to **Unlock controls?**.
3. Tap it again.

The back gesture still closes the video, and the end of the video unlocks the screen.
