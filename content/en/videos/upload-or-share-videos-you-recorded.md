1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Photos & Videos** > tap the album > tap the video.
2. If the bar at the top is hidden, tap the screen.
3. Tap the Share button. Email opens a new message with the video attached.
4. Address the message and send it.

Large videos may be too big for your email service. Videos cannot be uploaded to a cloud account from Photos & Videos.
