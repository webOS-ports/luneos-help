**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Videos** > tap a video.

The library shows every video in your device's storage, and on memory cards and USB sticks. Videos you stopped part way are at the top, under **Continue Watching**.

{if:unreleased}To find a video, type its name in **Search**. On a newer LuneOS build, Videos also searches your media servers: what they find is listed under **On** and the server's name ([learn how](/videos/watch-videos-from-a-media-server)). To sort, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Sort by Name**, **Sort by Date** or **Sort by Size**.
{if:!unreleased}To find a video, type its name in **Search**. To sort, open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** and tap **Sort by Name**, **Sort by Date** or **Sort by Size**.

Press and hold a video for **Play from the Start** or **Remove from Continue Watching**.

{if:unreleased}To watch videos from Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, PeerTube or a UPnP/DLNA media server, see [Add a media server account](/videos/add-a-media-server-account). To add videos from a computer, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).
{if:!unreleased}To add videos from a computer, see [Copy files between your device and your computer](/basics/copy-files-between-your-device-and-your-computer).
