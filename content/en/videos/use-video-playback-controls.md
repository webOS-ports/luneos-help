Tap the screen to show or hide the controls. They hide by themselves after a few seconds of playing, and stay while the video is paused.

- **Play/pause:** The button in the middle of the bottom row.
- **Back or forward 10 seconds:** The buttons on either side of play/pause.
- **Move anywhere in the video:** Drag the seek bar. The time played and the time left are on either side of it.
- **Speed:** The speed button, from 0.5x to 2x.
- **Audio track and subtitles:** The buttons at the left of the row. See [Use subtitles and audio tracks](/videos/use-subtitles-and-audio-tracks).
- **Fullscreen:** The fullscreen button shows or hides the status bar and gesture area.
- **Turn the picture:** On a tall screen, the turn button turns a wide video.
- **Lock:** The lock at the top right turns off the controls and taps, for when the device is in your hand or pocket. See [Lock the screen while watching a video](/videos/lock-the-screen-while-watching-a-video).

Double-tap to switch between the whole picture and filling the screen.

The Videos app remembers where you stopped. When you start again, tap **Start over** to go back to the beginning.
