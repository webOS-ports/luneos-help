**[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Videos** and open the **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)**. Tap any of these:

- **Sort by Name**, **Sort by Date** or **Sort by Size**.
- **Turn Wide Videos on Tall Screens:** A wide video is turned to fill a tall screen.
- **Read Video Lengths:** Videos reads the length of all your videos in the background. Otherwise it shows a video's length once you have played it. If reading a file stops the app, Videos says which file and turns this off.
- **Refresh:** Looks for new videos.
