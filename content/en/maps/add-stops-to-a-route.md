1. Find a place, or tap and hold the map to drop a pin.
2. Tap its marker, then tap the box that appears.
3. Tap **Add as stop**. The place becomes the last stop before your destination.
4. Do the same for other stops.

A route that is on the map changes by itself. Otherwise the directions form opens.

To remove a stop: Open the panel at the lower left of the screen, tap Directions, and tap the cross beside the stop.
