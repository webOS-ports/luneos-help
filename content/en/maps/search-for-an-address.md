1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Maps**.
2. Tap the search box at the top of the screen > enter an address > press Enter.
3. To do more with the place, tap its marker, then tap the box that appears:
   - To save it: Tap **Save place**.
   - To get directions: Tap **Route from here** or **Route to here**.
   - To send it: Tap **Share**.

To find a place you saved, see [Work with saved locations](/maps/work-with-saved-locations). Maps needs an internet connection to search.
