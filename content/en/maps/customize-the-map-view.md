1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Maps**.
2. Tap the handle at the lower left of the screen to open the panel. The buttons along its top are Map Type, Layers, Places, Directions, Settings and About.
3. Do any of the following:
   - To change the map: Tap Map Type > **OpenStreetMap**, **Humanitarian**, **OpenTopoMap** or **CyclOSM**.
   - To add an overlay: Tap Layers > turn **Transit** or **Bike** on or off.
   - To change the map provider: Tap Settings > **Map engine**. The app reloads. With Google Maps you also get other map types, **Traffic** and public transport routes.
   - To change the distance units: Tap Settings > **Units**.
4. Tap **Close**.

To mark a spot on the map, tap and hold it. A pin drops there; tap it to use it like any other place.

To clear the map, tap the cross beside the search box.
