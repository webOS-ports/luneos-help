1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Maps**.
2. Tap the search box at the top of the screen > enter the business name > press Enter.
3. Tap a marker on the map to see the place's name and address.
4. Tap that box for more: **Info**, **Save place**, **Share**, **Route from here**, **Route to here**.

To see all results in a list, open the panel at the lower left of the screen and tap Places > **Current**.

Maps searches OpenStreetMap unless you changed the map engine, so it finds what is in that database. It needs an internet connection.
