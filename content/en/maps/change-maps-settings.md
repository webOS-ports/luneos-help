1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Maps**.
2. Tap the handle at the lower left of the screen to open the panel, and tap Settings.
3. Change any of these:
   - **Map engine:** The provider of maps, search and directions: **OpenStreetMap** or **Google Maps**. The app reloads.
   - **Language:** The language of the map and the app. Restart Maps afterward.
   - **GPS update interval:** How often your position is updated.
   - **Units:** **Metric (km)** or **Imperial (mi)**.
4. Tap **Close**.

The help button beside a setting explains it.
