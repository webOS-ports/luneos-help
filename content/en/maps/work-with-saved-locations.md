To save a place:

1. Tap its marker on the map, then tap the box that appears.
2. Tap **Save place**.

To work with saved places:

1. Tap the handle at the lower left of the screen to open the panel > tap Places > **Saved**.
2. Tap a place to show it on the map.
3. Tap its marker, then tap the box that appears, and do any of the following:
   - To get directions: Tap **Route from here** or **Route to here**.
   - To send it: Tap **Share** > **Email** or **Copy**.
   - {if:modem}To send it in a message: Tap **Share** > **Message**.
   - To delete it: Tap **Remove from saved**.
