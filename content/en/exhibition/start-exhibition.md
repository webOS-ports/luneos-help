Do one of the following:

- **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Exhibition** > **Start Exhibition**.
- Place your device on an inductive charging dock, such as a Touchstone. If the screen is off, Exhibition starts. If the screen is on, Exhibition starts when the device has been inactive for a while.

Exhibition does not start during a call, and it does not start when you charge your device over USB.
