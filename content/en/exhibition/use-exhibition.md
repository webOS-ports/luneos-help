- Exhibition shows **Time**, plus a page for each app you checked in Exhibition settings ([learn how](/exhibition/customize-exhibition-settings)).
- To switch to another page, tap the page name in the upper-left corner of the screen and tap the page you want. On an app's page, you can also swipe left or right.
- If you checked no apps, **Time** is the only page. See [Which apps work in Exhibition?](/exhibition/which-apps-work-in-exhibition)
- When Exhibition starts again, it shows the page you used last, until the device or its shell restarts.
