1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Clock** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences**.
2. Under **Theme**, tap the current theme > **Analog 1**, **Analog 2**, or **Digital**.

   **Analog 1** and **Analog 2** are clock faces with hands. **Digital** shows the time as numbers.
3. Tap **Done**.

See Also [**Manage Clock**](/clock/manage-clock)
