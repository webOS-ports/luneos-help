1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Clock** > **[Menu](/basics/open-the-application-menu)** > **Preferences**.
2. Do any of the following:
   - To set whether alarms sound when sound is muted: Under **Alarm Sound**, tap the current setting next to **When Sounds is Muted** > **Play Alarm Sound** or **Mute Alarm Sound**.
   - To set how long you can snooze after an alarm goes off: Under **Snooze**, tap the current time > **5 Min**, **10 Min**, **15 Min**, or **20 Min**.
   - To change the clock display: Under **Theme**, tap the current theme > **Analog 1**, **Analog 2**, or **Digital** ([**learn how**](/clock/change-the-clock-theme)).
3. Tap **Done**.

To change the time: Open **Date & Time** (**[learn how](/date-and-time/use-date-and-time)**).
