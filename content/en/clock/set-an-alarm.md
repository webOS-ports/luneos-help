Before You Begin

Make sure sound is on and you can hear it. If you want alarms to sound while sound is muted, make sure **When Sounds is Muted** is set to **Play Alarm Sound** ([**learn how**](/clock/manage-clock)).

1. **[Open](/basics/open-an-application-in-the-launcher) Clock**.
2. Tap the alarm icon at the bottom of the screen > **New Alarm**.
3. Do the following:

   **Enabled:** Tap **On**/**Off**.

   **Alarm Name:** Enter a name for the alarm.

   **Occurs:** Tap the current setting > **Once**, **Daily**, **Weekdays**, or **Weekends**.

   **Time:** Tap to set the hour and minute for the alarm.

   **Sound:** Tap to choose the sound. The default is Alert.
4. Tap **Done**.

When an alarm sounds on your device, tap **Dismiss** to stop it or **Snooze** to have it sound again. The default snooze duration is 10 minutes, but you can change this to 5, 15, or 20 minutes ([**learn how**](/clock/manage-clock)).
